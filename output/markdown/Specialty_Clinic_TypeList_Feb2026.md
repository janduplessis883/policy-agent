## Appendix: North West London MSK Partnership (PDF page 17)

**Organisations shown:** North West London MSK Partnership; Cora Health; NHS North West London Musculoskeletal Service.  
**Form:** NWL MSK Referral Form - July 2025.  
**Provider:** Cora Health Services.  
**Version:** v5.0.

### Inclusion

The Service shall treat the following conditions (not an exhaustive list):

- Arthralgia (e.g. osteoarthritis), myalgia (small and large joint, soft tissue, muscular pain).
- Spinal pain (neck, back and thoracic spine & radicular pain).
- Chronic regional or widespread MSK pain (e.g. Fibromyalgia).
- MSK related soft tissue lesions (e.g., tendon injuries, ganglion, Dupuytren's contracture, trigger finger).
- Metabolic bone disorders (e.g., Osteoporosis).
- Peripheral nerve entrapments (e.g., carpal tunnel syndrome).
- Sports Injuries.
- Common MSK podiatric needs (biomechanics assessment & basic orthotics provided).

### Exclusions

Please do not refer the following patients:

- Emergency non-MSK presentations e.g. CES, MSCC, Spinal infection, Septic Arthritis, GCA, unstable acute fractures.
- Patients under the age of 16.
- Patients not registered with a GP in the commissioning boroughs (unless a non-contractual agreement is in place).
- Patients requiring home visits - please refer to local community teams.
- NHS England Prescribed Specialist Commissioning Services.
- Patients requiring non-MSK podiatry (e.g., nail surgery, diabetic foot, foot ulcers etc) or advanced / bespoke orthotics.
